Sun Salutation A
A timeless sequence. Each round takes about 3–4 minutes. Move with your breath — inhale to rise, exhale to fold. The sequence is simple enough that your mind can rest and follow the breath.
🌿 Recommended: 3–5 rounds
Find your mat
Stand at the top. Feet hip-width apart. Hands by your sides. Close your eyes for a moment.
🌬 3 deep breaths
Set your intention
Why are you here today? Let that be your anchor. You don't need to be anywhere else.
2
The Full Sequence
20 min3–5 rounds
▾
Breath is the guide. Every movement follows the breath. Lose the breath — pause. Slow everything down until you find it again.
Sun Salutation A — Step by Step
①
Mountain Pose (Tadasana) — stand tall, feet hip-width, arms by sides. Take 1 full breath.
Inhale
Upward Salute (Urdhva Hastasana) — raise arms overhead, lengthen spine, gaze softly up.
Exhale
Forward Fold (Uttanasana) — fold from hips, knees soft. Relax neck and shoulders.
Inhale
Half Lift (Ardha Uttanasana) — hands to shins or floor. Lengthen spine, chest forward.
Exhale
Step or jump back to Plank Pose — body in one straight line.
Exhale
Chaturanga Dandasana — lower slowly, elbows in. Mod: knees down.
Inhale
Upward Dog or Cobra — open chest, shoulders back, top of feet press down.
Exhale
Downward Facing Dog (Adho Mukha Svanasana) — hips high. Hold 5 breaths.
Inhale
Step or jump forward — come back to Half Lift.
Exhale
Forward Fold — surrender.
Inhale
Rise to standing — sweep arms overhead.
Exhale
Return to Mountain Pose — hands to heart. Rest here before the next round.
Adilla says: If Chaturanga is too much, just lower knees and rest in Child's Pose. The breath matters more than the shape.

Cool Down & Rest
7 minSavasana
▾
Child's Pose (Balasana)
Kneel, big toes together, sit back on heels. Stretch arms forward or rest them by your sides. Let the forehead meet the mat.
🌬 8 deep breaths
Seated Forward Fold (Paschimottanasana)
Extend legs long. Inhale lengthen spine, exhale fold forward. Hands to feet or shins. No forcing.
🌬 8 breaths
Supine Twist
Lie on back. Draw one knee to chest, guide it across the body. Arms wide. Gaze away from knees.
🌬 6 breaths each side
Savasana — Corpse Pose
Lie flat. Arms slightly away from body, palms up. Feet fall open. Close the eyes. Let everything go.
⏱ 3–5 minutes — do not skip this
Adilla says: Go home and be happy 😜 Your body absorbed 30 minutes of conscious movement. That is enough.

Sun Salutation B
Surya Namaskar B adds Chair Pose and Warrior I to the classic A sequence. A little more heat — still very accessible. Your legs and hips will thank you.
🌻 Recommended: 3–5 rounds
Mountain Pose — Stand tall
At the top of the mat. Feet hip-width. Eyes soft. Take 3 slow breaths to arrive fully.
🌬 3 breaths
Notice the difference from A
In B, you start with Chair Pose instead of Upward Salute. And you visit Warrior I twice — once per side — inside each round. That's it.
2
The Full Sequence
20 min3–5 rounds
▾
One round = all 22 steps. Warrior I visits both sides in each round. Take your time — especially in Down Dog and Warrior I.
Sun Salutation B — Step by Step
①
Mountain Pose (Tadasana)
Inhale
Chair Pose (Utkatasana) — bend knees, hips back, arms reach overhead.
Exhale
Forward Fold (Uttanasana)
Inhale
Half Lift (Ardha Uttanasana)
Exhale
Step or jump back to Plank Pose
Exhale
Chaturanga Dandasana — Mod: knees down
Inhale
Upward Facing Dog or Cobra
Exhale
Downward Facing Dog
Inhale
Step right foot forward → Warrior I (Virabhadrasana I) — back heel grounded, arms reach up. Hold 1 breath.
Exhale
Hands down, step back to Plank
Exhale
Chaturanga
Inhale
Upward Facing Dog
Exhale
Downward Facing Dog
Inhale
Step left foot forward → Warrior I — hold 1 breath.
Exhale
Hands down, step back to Plank
Exhale
Chaturanga
Inhale
Upward Facing Dog
Exhale
Downward Facing Dog — stay 5 breaths
Inhale
Step or jump forward
Exhale
Forward Fold
Inhale
Chair Pose — arms up, sink the hips
Exhale
Mountain Pose — return, hands to heart. Rest before the next round.
Adilla says: Warrior I is a power pose. Ground the back heel fully, square the hips as best you can, and breathe into the chest as you reach up.

Standing Postures
12 minStrength
▾
Warrior I (Virabhadrasana I)
Front knee over ankle. Back heel grounded. Hips square forward. Arms strong overhead.
🌬 5 breaths each side
Warrior II (Virabhadrasana II)
Open hips to the side. Front knee tracks over toes. Gaze over front fingers. Arms parallel to floor.
🌬 5 breaths each side
Extended Side Angle (Utthita Parsvakonasana)
From Warrior II. Forearm to thigh or hand to block/floor. Top arm extends overhead. Long line from back foot to fingertips.
🌬 5 breaths each side
Triangle (Trikonasana)
Straight front leg. Reach forward then hinge. Hand to shin, block, or floor. Top arm reaches for the sky.
🌬 5 breaths each side

Balancing & Peak
10 minFocus
▾
Drishti — fixed gaze point. Pick one spot. Balancing postures are a moving meditation. Falls are part of the practice.
Tree Pose (Vrksasana)
Root one foot. Place the other at calf or inner thigh — not the knee. Hands to heart or overhead.
🌬 8 breaths each side
Warrior III (Virabhadrasana III)
From standing, hinge forward, one leg lifts behind. Body makes a T-shape. Arms forward or by sides.
🌬 5 breaths each side
Peak Pose — Your choice
Choose what your body needs today: Dancer's Pose, Half Moon, Side Plank, or simply hold Warrior III longer.
🌬 5 breaths each side

Savasana
5 minutes · do not skip
Lie flat on your back.
Feet fall open naturally. Arms slightly away from the body, palms face up.

Close the eyes. Breathe naturally.

Allow the body to completely receive the practice.
There is nothing to do. Only this moment.
End with a gentle seated closing.
